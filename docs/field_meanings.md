# Understanding the AIESEC Exchange Funnel

The columns in the dataset represent the sequential stages of the **AIESEC Exchange Funnel**, tracking an applicant's journey from initial interest to project completion. Similar to an e-commerce conversion funnel, candidates progress through these six distinct operational statuses in strict chronological order.

---

### Stage Breakdown

#### 1. `applied` (Lead Stage — Top of Funnel)
* **Definition:** An applicant creates an EXPA account and submits an application for a specific exchange opportunity (e.g., Global Volunteer, Global Teacher, or Global Talent).
* **Operational Context:** Represents broad marketing reach and high lead volume with a low barrier to entry. Many leads at this stage do not complete screening or advance further.

#### 2. `matched` (Shortlisting Stage — Mid Funnel)
* **Definition:** The hosting entity or company reviews the candidate's CV and application, shortlisting them for formal screening or interviews.
* **Operational Context:** Serves as the initial screening gate, confirming that the candidate meets the basic skills, language, and background requirements for the role.

#### 3. `accepted` (Offer Stage — Mid Funnel)
* **Definition:** The applicant passes the interview/assessment process, receives a formal offer from the hosting entity, and accepts it on EXPA.
* **Operational Context:** Confirms mutual intent between both parties, though administrative and financial commitments remain pending.

#### 4. `approved` (Conversion Stage — Core Metric)
* **Definition:** The applicant officially signs the exchange contract, pays the required exchange fees, and fulfills all pre-departure administrative criteria.
* **Operational Context:** **The primary KPI and revenue conversion metric for AIESEC operations.** Reaching this stage locks the participant into the exchange pipeline.

#### 5. `realized` (Execution Stage — Downstream Outcome)
* **Definition:** The participant physically arrives in the host country and completes their first day on the project or internship.
* **Operational Context:** Measures actual delivery. This metric inherently exhibits a **time lag** (typically 1–3 months) relative to `approved` due to visa processing, flight bookings, and travel arrangements.

#### 6. `completed` (Post-Exchange Stage — Finalization)
* **Definition:** The participant finishes the exchange duration and completes the required end-of-project evaluations and standards on EXPA.
* **Operational Context:** Indicates successful program completion. Drop-offs here are typically administrative, occurring when participants omit post-project surveys upon returning home.
